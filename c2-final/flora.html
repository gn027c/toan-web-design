Submerged & Emergent Wetland Flora

Plants within the Dong Thap Muoi basin exhibit remarkable morphological specializations, allowing them to withstand deep annual submergence as well as parched drought conditions.

  • Melaleuca Cajuputi (Cajuput / Tram Gio)

    Family: Myrtaceae • Habitat: Acid-sulfate flooded soils

    Possesses multilayered, spongy bark that breathes under water and stores reserves against seasonal dry spells. Flowers provide a primary nectar source for native wild honeybees.

  • Nelumbo Nucifera (Sacred Lotus / Sen Hong)

    Family: Nelumbonaceae • Habitat: Shallow alluvial mudflats

    Features superhydrophobic micro-textured leaves that continuously clean themselves. Serves as a keystone organism filtering nitrogen and phosphates from the watershed.

  • Nymphaea Pubescens (Hairy Water Lily / Sung Do)

    Family: Nymphaeaceae • Habitat: Slow-flowing freshwater canals

    Deeply rooted in alluvial silt with cordate floating leaves that shade underwater fauna and stabilize oxygen concentrations across the diurnal cycle.
