Welcome to the Vam Co Wetland Basin

The Dong Thap Muoi wetland region, spanning Long An Province and neighboring territories of Southern Vietnam, is one of Southeast Asia's most ecologically significant floodplains. Governed by alternating wet monsoons and dry seasons, this dynamic aquatic landscape nurtures centuries-old submerged forests and vast lotus marshlands.

Our digital preservation initiative documents the delicate balance between freshwater hydrology, indigenous acid-sulfate soil conditions, and endemic flora that have sustained local livelihoods and ecosystems for generations.

Core Ecological Pillars

The sanctuary's long-term conservation strategy focuses on three foundational ecological priorities:

  • Hydrological Regulation: Maintaining seasonal retention basins to temper peak flooding and replenish subterranean water tables.
  • Soil Regeneration: Leveraging the deep, fibrous root mats of melaleuca stands to suppress acid sulfate toxicity and enrich topsoils.
  • Community-Led Stewardship: Fostering sustainable eco-tourism practices that empower local residents while actively protecting wildlife corridors.
