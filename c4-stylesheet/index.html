Sustainable Wetland Agro-Forestry

Balancing Melaleuca conservation, alluvial organic agriculture, and eco-tourism along the Southern Vàm Cỏ river basin in Long An province.

01. Melaleuca Forest Restoration

Preserving natural peat swamps and endemic cajuputi tree populations to maintain freshwater flood moderation and subterranean water tables.

02. Organic Rice Cultivation

Implementing chemical-free floating rice agriculture attuned to seasonal alluvial silt influx during the Mekong autumn inundation.

03. Waterways Eco-Trekking

Low-impact traditional wooden boat circuits exploring mangrove networks, bird sanctuaries, and traditional craft villages.

Ecological Zoning Matrix

Alluvial Buffer Strip
