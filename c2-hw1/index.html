About Tan Lap Wetland Sanctuary

Located in the heart of the Dong Thap Muoi region in Moc Hoa District, Long An Province, the Tan Lap Floating Village is a pristine eco-tourism sanctuary spanning over 135 hectares. The sanctuary is renowned for its majestic submerged melaleuca forest (cajuput forest), intersected by a unique 5-kilometer elevated walking path.

Key Wetland Botanical Species

The wetland supports a rich tapestry of native freshwater species adapted to seasonal alluvial flooding:

  • Melaleuca cajuputi (Cajuput Trees): Dense, resilient stands that stabilize alluvial soils and produce medicinal aromatic oils.
  • Nelumbo nucifera (Sacred Lotus): Blooming across seasonal floodplains, providing natural filtration for the watershed.
  • Nymphaea pubescens (Water Lilies): Night-blooming aquatic plants that nurture benthic freshwater macroinvertebrates.
  • Eleocharis dulcis (Water Chestnuts): Vital foraging grounds for migratory waterfowl and aquatic birds.

Environmental Stewardship & Conservation

Preserving this tranquil wetland is paramount for maintaining biodiversity across the Southern Plain. Local rangers and environmental researchers continuously monitor water salinity, fish spawning grounds, and avian migratory cycles to maintain an optimal ecological balance throughout the monsoon and dry seasons.
