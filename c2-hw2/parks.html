Sanctuary Network

The Vam Co Dong and Vam Co Tay river basins create one of the most productive deltaic freshwater networks in Southern Vietnam. The sanctuaries below protect critical biodiversity corridors connecting inland marshlands to coastal mangrove buffers.

Protected Nature Parks (Two-Column CSS Grid)

Vam Co Dong Wetland Buffer

A protected freshwater ecosystem characterized by winding alluvial channels, dense reeds, and natural water retention basins that prevent saltwater intrusion.

Dong Thap Muoi Botanical Reserve

A regional ecological station dedicated to seed banking indigenous aquatic flora, reforestation of cajuput trees, and wetland soil conservation.

Ecological Initiatives (CSS Flexbox)

Water Quality Monitoring

Continuous pH and salinity tracking across river stations to safeguard freshwater irrigation and aquaculture.

Indigenous Tree Nursery

Cultivating over 50,000 native Melaleuca and mangrove seedlings annually for community reforestation drives.

Public Ecology Education

Interactive nature workshops for local students to foster environmental stewardship and river conservation.
