Habitat Overview

Tan Lap Floating Village is a protected eco-sanctuary located in Moc Hoa district, Long An province. Spanning over 135 hectares of pristine Melaleuca forest, the area serves as a critical refuge for migratory waterfowl, aquatic life, and rare wetland flora during the annual flood season.

Habitat Zones (Two-Column CSS Grid)

Elevated Forest Trail

A 5-kilometer elevated stone walkway winding through towering Melaleuca trees, allowing visitors and researchers to observe dense mosses and ferns without disturbing the fragile aquatic forest floor.

Rach Rung Waterway Channels

Natural water arteries navigable by traditional wooden sampans, fringed by wild lotus, water hyacinth, and floating grass mats that filter sediment from monsoon waters.

Conservation Focus Areas (CSS Flexbox)

Aquatic Plant Conservation

Preserving endangered native lotus and floating vegetation that maintain water clarity and balance the wetland food web.

Avian Nesting Zones

Designated undisturbed zones providing shelter and breeding canopy for storks, herons, and migratory kingfishers.

Community Agroforestry

Empowering local communities with sustainable honey harvesting and regulated ecotourism to protect forest reserves.
