Curator's Introduction

This botanical registry documents indigenous vegetation adapted to the seasonal flood pulses and alluvial soils of the lower Mekong basin. Using modern responsive layouts, the specimens below highlight key morphological and ecological features vital for environmental monitoring.

Botanical Attributes & Morphological Notes

  • Stilt Root Systems: Provide aerating lenticels above water levels to survive prolonged inundation.
  • Hydrophobic Leaf Cuticles: Self-cleaning surfaces that resist silt deposition during flash flood phases.
  • Aerenchyma Tissue: Spongy internal air channels allowing efficient gas exchange from leaves to roots.
  • Aromatic Essential Terpenes: Volatile organic compounds that deter insect pests and resist fungal spores.
