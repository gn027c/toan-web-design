Curator's Overview

This botanical registry documents indigenous vegetation adapted to the seasonal flood pulses and alluvial soils of the lower Mekong basin. Using modern CSS grid and flexbox, the specimens below highlight key morphological and ecological features vital for environmental monitoring.

Featured Botanical Specimens (Two-Column CSS Grid)

Melaleuca Cajuputi

Family: Myrtaceae

Evergreen paperbark tree thriving in acidic waterlogged soils, stabilizing canal embankments and producing essential cajuput oils.

Nelumbo Nucifera

Family: Nelumbonaceae

Iconic emergent aquatic perennial with peltate leaves and fragrant blossoms, crucial for waterfowl nesting and traditional wetland agriculture.

Nymphaea Pubescens

Family: Nymphaeaceae

Hairy water lily with floating cordate foliage that provides benthic shade, reduces algae blooms, and cools waterway temperatures.

Rhizophora Apiculata

Family: Rhizophoraceae

Stilt-rooted mangrove protecting estuary waterways against seasonal floods and preventing alluvial bank erosion.
